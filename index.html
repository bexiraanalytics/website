02 / What working with BEXIRA looks like

From first brief
to delivery.

Here's exactly what happens after you reach out

  1. Send a short project brief

    Tell us about your business, the problem you want solved, and what data you have. It takes about 3 to 5 minutes.

  2. Discovery call

    A 20 to 30 minute audio call, video only if you prefer. We talk through the problem and what your data can answer. If we aren't the right fit, we'll say so.

  3. Proposal

    You receive a fixed price, a clear list of deliverables, a timeline, and what we need from you.

  4. Payment confirms your start

    Once the proposal is agreed, a 50% deposit payment is made. This secures your project and start date.

  5. Your project begins

    We clean and analyse your data, then deliver your report or dashboard with a walkthrough so you can use it yourself. This happens only after full payment has been confirmed

06 / FAQ

Questions,
answered.

What kind of businesses do you work with?

Growing businesses with sales, customer or product data — especially e-commerce and retail, though we take on other data-rich businesses too.

Do I need to already have clean data?

No. Cleaning and structuring messy data is part of what we do — it's usually the first step, not a prerequisite.

What tools do you use?

Power BI, Excel, SQL, Python and Tableau, depending on what fits the project and how you want to keep using the output afterward.

How long does a typical project take?

It depends on scope, but most dashboard and analysis projects take one to three weeks from data handoff to delivery.

Is my business data kept confidential?

Yes. Your data and findings are treated as confidential and are never shared, reused or referenced without your permission.

What do I actually receive at the end?

A decision-ready dashboard, a written summary of what the data shows, and clear recommendations you can act on — not just charts.
