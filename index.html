02 / What working with BEXIRA looks like

From first brief
to delivery.

Here's exactly what happens after you reach out.

  1. Send a short project brief

    Tell us about your business, the problem you want solved, and what data you have. It takes about 3 to 5 minutes.

  2. Discovery call

    A 20 to 30 minute audio call, video only if you prefer. We talk through the problem and what your data can answer. If we aren't the right fit, we'll say so.

  3. Proposal

    You receive a fixed price, a clear list of deliverables, a timeline, and what we need from you.

  4. Payment confirms your start

    Once the proposal is agreed, payment secures your project and start date: in full for a Signal Review, or 50% for a Profit Review + Dashboard. The remaining 50% is due before the walkthrough and the handover of files.

  5. Your project begins

    We clean and analyse your data, then deliver your report or dashboard with a walkthrough so you can use it yourself. Delivery follows the final payment.

06 / FAQ

Questions,
answered.

Why “from” pricing?

Because scope changes with your data. A single clean sales export is a different job from five years across three channels — and quoting one number for both would either overcharge the small job or lose money on the large one. The “from” figure is the floor for the standard scope described on each card. After a short discovery call you get one fixed price, in writing, before any work starts. No hourly billing. No scope surprises halfway through.

What kind of businesses do you work with?

E-commerce and retail brands, mainly in Nigeria, doing roughly ₦60M to ₦600M a year with sales, customer or product data. We work with other data-rich businesses when the problem is a close fit — tell us what you have and we will say honestly whether we can help.

Do I need to already have clean data?

Not perfectly clean. Standard cleaning and checking is included in every project. If your data needs heavy repair, we will tell you before work starts and quote that part separately.

What tools do you use?

Power BI, Excel, SQL, Python, depending on what fits the project and how you want to keep using the output afterward.

How long does a typical project take?

7 business days for a Signal Review and 3 weeks for a Profit Review + Dashboard. A Growth Partner retainer runs month to month, with a six-month minimum.

Is my business data kept confidential?

Yes. Your data and findings are treated as confidential and are never shared, reused or referenced without your permission.

What do I actually receive at the end?

It depends on the tier. A Signal Review gives you a written diagnosis, three prioritised recommendations and a 30-minute walkthrough call. A Profit Review + Dashboard adds an interactive dashboard and the editable files. A Growth Partner retainer adds a monthly review and ongoing support.
